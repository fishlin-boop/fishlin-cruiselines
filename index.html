Important information regarding ticket processing, boarding, and code of conduct.

How Tickets and Boarding Work

  • Booking Confirmation: Once you complete a reservation, you receive a unique booking reference code (e.g., #FC-XXXX).
  • Virtual Boarding Passes: Boarding passes are issued at the virtual port terminal desk 2 hours before departure time.
  • All-Inclusive vs. Pay-Per-Use: Suite reservations cover your stateroom and basic lodging. Dining, spa treatments, arcade tokens, and special event passes require separate purchases or onboard account billing.
  • Network Integration: Tickets link directly with partner virtual airlines and hotel networks for seamless travel itineraries.

Fishlin Cruiselines Safety & Conduct Rules

  • Life Jacket Drill: All passengers must participate in the mandatory virtual safety briefing prior to ship departure.
  • Deck Etiquette: Running is strictly prohibited on the pool decks. Proper footwear must be worn in all dining areas.
  • Quiet Hours: Quiet hours are enforced across all stateroom corridors starting at 11:00 PM nightly.
  • Simulation Code: Respect fellow virtual travelers and crew members. Exploits or disruptive behavior in simulation areas will result in immediate disembarkation.
